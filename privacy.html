Yalla Hajj — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Yalla Hajj, a companion app for Umrah and Hajj, is a personal, non-commercial project published by Djamil Mbodj, an individual living in France ("we"). We are the controller of the personal data described below.

Contact for any privacy question or request: djamilmbodj470@gmail.com.

For privacy and legal correspondence, please contact us at the e-mail address above. No postal address is displayed on this page.

This policy applies to the Yalla Hajj iPhone app, including its beta version distributed through Apple TestFlight.

2. In short

  • You can create an account to use interactive features, including community features.
  • Many features work on your device only. Your Quran notes, memorisation progress, tasbih, Umrah progress, and your offers and needs in this version are not sent to our servers.
  • Community features need data on our servers and share some information with the people you interact with: group chats, prayer hosting and visits, Quran recitation sessions, and calls between users.
  • Data that may reveal your religious beliefs is handled with care: most of the app works on your device, and the community features are used only with your explicit consent, asked in a short dialog the first time you open one. You can turn it off at any time in the app, without deleting your account (section 3.3).
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data.
  • The app does not record the sound or images of calls.

3. Data, purposes and legal bases

3.1 Account

  • Data: e-mail address, password, first name and last name. If you use the community features, we also record the version and the date of the consent described in section 3.3.
  • Password: it is sent over an encrypted connection to our authentication provider. The app does not store it.
  • Automatic technical session: at first launch, before you sign up, the app creates an anonymous technical session. It has a random identifier, and no name or e-mail.
  • Purpose: to create, secure and operate your account.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract (Terms of Use).

3.2 Optional information

All fields below are optional.

  • Personalisation: date of birth, country and spoken languages are saved with your account. Your interests (for example Quran, prayer, giving) stay on your device only and are not sent to us.
  • Quran recitation sessions: display name, languages, level, presentation message and the country you choose. An optional self-declared gender is used only to offer same-gender matching if you ask for it. Other users never see it: only you can read this field.
  • Prayer hosting: host name, type of space, who you can welcome, capacity and facilities, and the exact address and position of the place you offer.
  • Legal basis: your consent. You give it by filling in the field. You can withdraw it by clearing the field where the app lets you edit it, or by writing to us.

3.3 Data that may reveal your religious beliefs

Data that reveals religious beliefs is specially protected (Article 9 of the GDPR). We limit it as follows.

  • On your device only: the Umrah guide, the Quran and Mushaf, duas, memorisation, tasbih, prayer settings and your interests stay on your iPhone. We do not keep them on our servers.
  • Community features: Connect & Meet, Prayer Nearby and Quran recitation sessions store and share, on our servers, data that may reveal your religious practice (for example the activities you join, prayer hosting and visits, recitation sessions). We process this data only with your explicit consent:
    • the first time you open one of these features, a short dialog asks for it ("Allow" or "Not now");
    • before you tap "Allow", nothing of these features is sent to our servers, and our server itself refuses community data from an account without consent;
    • the version of the wording and the date of your consent are recorded on your account by our server; the app itself cannot change this record;
    • if you choose "Not now", the rest of the app works normally, and the dialog appears again the next time you open a community feature.
  • Withdrawal, as simple as the consent: turn off Community features in You › Account, or write to us. The withdrawal is recorded on your account first: from that moment our server refuses any new community data from your account, on all your devices (messages, files, "online" status). We then erase your community data from our servers, as described in section 4 for account deletion, and only that data: your account, your profile, your sign-in, your other files and data, and the rest of the app are kept. You can turn it on again later.
  • Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

The app asks for your location only while you use it. It does not request access in the background. You can refuse; most features then let you choose a place by hand.

  • Voice notes are recorded only when you press the record button.
  • Reporting and blocking are available in Connect & Meet (activity page and member profile), in prayer visits and in Quran recitation sessions. We read reports and record the decision taken for each one.
  • Automatic content filter: texts shared in community features are checked by our server against a list of offensive words when they are written, and refused if they contain one. Nothing is stored about a refused text.

3.6 Calls

  • What we store: for calls between users, we store the participants, the type of call, start and end times and the status of the call.
  • What we do not store: the app does not record and does not store the sound or images of calls.
  • How a call travels: in this beta, calls between Connect & Meet members go directly between the participants' devices. To find a direct path, the devices may contact a connection server (STUN), which only sees their IP address. The other participant's device therefore receives your IP address, which can reveal an approximate location. No relay server is used in this beta, so a call may fail on some networks.
  • Quran recitation sessions: live audio is not available in this beta. The app tells you so on the session screen.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract.

3.7 Data stored only on your device

In this version, the following data is stored only on your iPhone:

  • offers, needs, handover passes and their photos;
  • Quran notes, favourites and memorisation;
  • tasbih, Umrah progress, prayer settings and preferences.

Offers and needs are not visible to other users in this version. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3.8 Technical data

  • IP address: the services you connect to receive your IP address. These are our hosting provider, the connection server used for calls, the other participant in a call (section 3.6) and the services listed in section 5.
  • Logs: our hosting provider keeps technical logs of requests and errors, which include IP addresses, for about one day.
  • No analytics: the app does not send us analytics, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • TestFlight: if you test through Apple TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy, and shares them with us.

4. Deleting your account

  • In the app: You tab, Account section, Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is carried out immediately.
  • By e-mail: you can also ask us to delete your account by writing to the address in section 1.
  • What is deleted:
    • your account, your profile, your optional information and your consent record;
    • your prayer-hosting and recitation data, your call details and your reviews;
    • the files you shared: photos, voice notes, files and group pictures;
    • your reports and blocks, and the reports that concern you.
  • Shared spaces are kept for the other users:
    • your messages in group chats are removed; other members see "This message was deleted" in their place, and their own messages are kept;
    • an activity you organised without other participants is deleted; an upcoming activity with participants is cancelled, and they are told in its chat; a past one stays visible to its participants;
    • in a kept activity, your name, its description, photos and meeting note are removed, and an address visible only to members is reduced to the neighbourhood;
    • prayer visits and recitation sessions in progress are closed and the other person is told; history lines involving you are deleted;
    • mentions of you in other people's messages stay as written but no longer link to your account.
  • What remains: for a kept activity, its title, category, date, time and place, and a technical identifier that no longer leads to any account, name or profile.
  • Backups: with our current hosting plan, no automatic database backup is made. Technical logs disappear after about one day.

5. Recipients and international transfers

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd.): our hosting provider and processor (database, authentication, file storage, server functions).
    • Your data is hosted in the European Union, in Stockholm, Sweden.
    • Some of Supabase's sub-processors may be located outside the European Union. Supabase's data processing agreement incorporates the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914) for such transfers, and Supabase publishes its list of sub-processors.
  • Apple: the app is distributed through the App Store and TestFlight, and uses Apple's iOS services (for example geocoding). Apple is based in the United States.
  • Independent services reached by the app, which receive your IP address and the content request. Their own privacy policies apply, and some are located outside the European Union:
    • Quran content: Quran Foundation and QuranEnc, reached through our server, which does not transmit your identity; mp3quran.net and Tarteel, reached directly by the app;
    • maps and places: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Overpass and Komoot Photon.
  • Links you open: Google Maps, Apple Maps, Google Calendar, quran.com, sunnah.com and official sites. Their own policies apply.

6. What other users see

  • Prayer hosting:
    • a host sees the guest's first name, party size, needs, approximate distance and rating;
    • the guest sees the host's name and picture, and the exact address once the visit is confirmed.
  • Connect & Meet:
    • signed-in users see the organiser's name and picture and the number of participants;
    • members see the member list (first name, picture, role), messages, read receipts and online status.
  • Recitation sessions: display name, level, languages and message, but never your gender.
  • Reviews: your first name and picture.

8. Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. You can also withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

  • Device permissions: iOS permissions (location, camera, photos, microphone) can be revoked in your iPhone settings.
  • Requests: write to djamilmbodj470@gmail.com. We answer within one month; this period may be extended in the cases provided for by law.
  • Complaints: you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country. In France, this is the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

9. Security

  • Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database access rules are designed so that each piece of data is available only to the people allowed to see it.
  • Shared files are kept in private storage.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs, we will notify the authorities and the people concerned when the law requires it.

10. Young users

Yalla Hajj does not impose a general minimum age of 18. However:

  • Under 15: if you are under 15 (or under the age at which your country's law lets you consent alone), you may create an account only with the agreement of one of your parents or legal guardians. They must also give, together with you, the consent described in section 3.3.
  • Not for young children: the app is not designed for children under 13. If we learn that an account was created without the required parental agreement, we delete it.
  • TestFlight: participation remains subject to Apple's conditions for TestFlight.

The app does not verify users' age.

11. Changes

We publish any change to this policy on this page, with a new effective date at the top. For a significant change, we will try to inform you in advance by e-mail.

9. Sécurité

  • Les échanges entre l'application et nos serveurs sont chiffrés (HTTPS).
  • Les règles d'accès de la base de données sont conçues pour que chaque donnée ne soit accessible qu'aux personnes autorisées.

Aucun système n'est totalement sûr. En cas de violation de données, nous informerons les autorités et les personnes concernées lorsque la loi l'exige.
